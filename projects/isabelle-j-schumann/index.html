Isabelle J Schumann

Hi, my name is Isabelle. I’m a digital artist and graphic designer. I mostly do illustrations, logos, character turnarounds, and much more like; web designing with html and css code. I also work with photography and photo editing software. I also worked with video editing software as well as worked on 2d, and 3d animation, as well as kinetic typography. I’ve also dabbled in music creation software, and content creation for my socials as hobbies i do on the side here and there. Other hobbies include: sewing, arts and crafts, biking, swimming, and character designing.

I began as a kid with a dream, a box of crayons and a dream for art. I’ve always looked up to artists, animators, and designers ever since I was 7. I’ve always had an eye for artistic genius for fantastic paintings like “starry night”, “american gothic”, and “mona lisa.” The technique to make such pieces always fascinated me. Other things that inspired me at a young age were early 2000’s movies from Pixar, Warner Brothers, and paramonte. It was a time when computer animation was still in its infancy. I’ve always loved the blend of multiple art forms together in animation such as a combo of 2d, 3d, computer, traditional, and stop motion in one medium. Examples include: “Coraline”, Brad Birds’ “The Iron Giant”, and even music videos.

I’ve always strived to be the best I can be when I make art, music, projects, video media, etc.And I will continue to make the best designs and pieces of artwork for the foreseeable future.This is my lifestyle, life work, life blood, and life legacy.

What I Do:

I’m a Graphic and Website Designer. I went to F.I.R.S.T. Institute to pursue a career in graphic and web design to improve my skills in illustration, basic design, and artworks. I’ve taken most interest in illustration, logo, poster, product design, product photography, and basic animations.For example designing a character is one thing, but animating one is another. That is why I create character model sheets, to keep character’s designs consistent in different types of animations like; traditional, or computer animation. I’m more into the traditional 2d animation because I grew up with classic hand drawn animations, but I've experimented with blender and maya character modeling programs. I prefer blender because it is more user friendly and it's free to use! How cool is that? Other programs, not so much.

Even though you have to pay for a monthly subscription to use the Adobe suite, it is still worth it because, you have full access to programs such as illustrator, photoshop,after effects, indesign, etc. but there is one recent free program known as Figma, that is a perfect alternative to Adobe illustrator, if the monthly Adobe suite is not within your budget.

If you are a Florida Resident, or out of state, and are interested in attending F.I.R.S.T. Institute, I highly Recommend their online courses. And the best part is they only have three programs, and each one is only 10 months long. This will help you get out there in the industry space much faster than traditional 4 to 3 year schools. And if I were you, especially if you are a beginner, I would definitely look into it.

Software I Use For Graphic Design:

The most common software that I use is the full adobe suite as well as other editing software and programs. These include: Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop, Figma, Github, Adobe After Effects, Adobe Media encoder, Adobe lightroom, and Adobe Indesign.Other programs I use on the side are: Clip Studio Paint, Blender, Garageband, Medibang Paint Pro, Aseprite, and Capcut.
